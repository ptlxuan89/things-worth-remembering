This started with a DISC assessment I took on January 7, 2026. I expected
another forgettable workplace personality test. Instead, the results put
words to something I'd been feeling for a while — and I've been sitting with
them ever since.

I've spent years learning how to show up for others.

Be reliable. Make collaboration easy. Step in when something needs to get
done.

Those habits served me well. But they also came with an assumption: *if I
can do something, I should.*

The result isn't always burnout. It's saying yes before thinking, carrying
work that isn't really mine, and spending energy proving I can help instead
of focusing where I can create the most impact.

Energy is a resource, too. I'm still learning that.

The goal isn't to become less caring or less collaborative. It's to be more
intentional:

- Just because you can carry more doesn't mean you should.
- Being reliable doesn't mean being constantly available.
- Protecting your energy isn't selfish. It's how you sustain your best work.

For me, that means a little more directness, a few more boundaries, and a
little less guilt about taking up space.

The strengths that got us here aren't things to outgrow. They're things to
protect. ✨
